Scape — Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Summary: You can permanently delete your Scape account and associated data directly within the app, in just a few steps.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Scape app and sign in to your account
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top right of the home screen)
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

If you're unable to access the app, email support.scapeapp@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and request deletion — we'll process it manually.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account record, including your unique user identifier (Firebase Auth UID)
  • Your credit balance and total render count
  • Your account creation timestamp and subscription tier
  • Your sign-in provider information
  • Your free-trial device record (Android ID), if applicable

What Is Retained, and For How Long

  • Purchase records: Retained for 7 years for legal and tax compliance, as required by law. This data is disassociated from your active account access but may still be linked to your purchase history for compliance purposes.
  • Locally stored renders and design history: These are stored only on your own device, not on our servers, so account deletion does not automatically remove them. To remove locally stored renders, uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in your device settings.

We do not store your room photos or generated renders in our cloud database, and we do not store your email address or display name — so there is no server-side copy of this content to delete beyond what's listed above.

Questions

Contact support.scapeapp@gmail.com with any questions about account deletion or data retention.
