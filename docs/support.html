Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use Scape?

No. You can start using Scape immediately as a guest with no sign-up required. You receive an anonymous session and can generate renders right away. If you later sign in with Google or create an account, your credits and history are linked to your permanent profile.

How do credits work?

Each AI remodel render costs 1 credit. New accounts start with 3 free credits. You can purchase additional credit packs at any time from within the app.

What credit packs are available?

Starter: 10 credits $3.99 | Standard: 20 credits $6.99 | Renovation: 60 credits $13.99 | Pro: 150 credits $24.99

Where are my photos and renders stored?

Your privacy is important to us. Room photos you upload are processed in real time by our AI and are never stored on our servers. Your generated renders are saved locally on your device up to your last 50 renders so you can view them offline. They are not uploaded to our cloud database.

How do I view my design history offline?

Scape automatically caches your last 50 renders on your device so they load instantly without an internet connection. Just open the app and navigate to your history.

How do I clear my design history?

Go to Settings then Clear History in the app. This removes your locally stored renders from your device. This action cannot be undone.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by the app store you purchased through. For Android visit Google Play Order History. For iOS visit Apple Report a Problem. You can also email us at support.scapeapp@gmail.com.

My render did not generate correctly — what do I do?

If a render fails and your credit was deducted, our system automatically restores it. If you believe a credit was incorrectly deducted, email us at support.scapeapp@gmail.com with your account email.

My credits are not showing after a purchase — what do I do?

Credits are usually applied instantly. If they have not appeared within 5 minutes, close and reopen the app. If the issue persists, email us at support.scapeapp@gmail.com with your account email and purchase receipt.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings then Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes your account profile, credit balance, and all cloud-stored account data. This cannot be undone. Alternatively email us at support.scapeapp@gmail.com and we will process the deletion within 30 days.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Photos you upload are processed in real time and never stored on our servers. Your generated renders stay on your device. Only your account metadata is stored in our secure cloud database. We do not sell your personal data. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

What types of rooms can I remodel?

Scape works with any interior space including bathrooms, kitchens, living rooms, bedrooms, and home offices. Simply upload a clear photo of your space and describe the remodel style you want.

How do I request my personal data?

Email us at support.scapeapp@gmail.com with the subject line Data Request. We will respond within 30 days.
