Scape — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 | Effective: August 4, 2026

Summary: Scape is an AI-powered home remodeling app. Your room photos and generated renders are stored locally on your device, not on our servers. We collect only minimal account data. We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your account at any time from within the app.

1. Who We Are

Scape is developed and operated by Alysia Anderson and David Anderson, operating as AndersonAI.io. Scape is an AI-powered interior design and home remodeling application available on Android and iOS.

Contact: support.scapeapp@gmail.com

2. How You Access Scape

2.1 Guest / Anonymous Access

You can use Scape immediately without creating an account. Guest users receive an automatic anonymous Firebase session. No email address or personal information is required.

  • Your design history is stored locally on your device only
  • If your session ends without signing in, your history remains on your device until it expires (after 30 days), you uninstall the app, or you clear the app's storage
  • If you later sign in, your credits and account are linked to your permanent profile

2.2 Google Sign-In

You may sign in using your Google account via Firebase Authentication. We receive your email address, display name, and profile photo as provided by Google.

2.3 Email and Password

You may create an account using your email address and a password via Firebase Authentication.

3. Information We Collect

Your room photos and AI-generated renders are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to or stored on our servers or cloud database.

3.1 Account Information

If you create an account, we store in Firebase Firestore:

  • Unique user identifier (Firebase Auth UID)
  • Credit balance
  • Total render count
  • Account creation timestamp
  • Subscription tier
  • Sign-in provider (e.g. Google, or anonymous guest)
  • A flag indicating whether your account was linked to a device that had already claimed a free trial (free-trial abuse prevention only — see below)

We do NOT store your room photos or generated renders in our cloud database, and we do NOT store your email address or display name.

Free-trial device check: When you claim your free trial credits on an Android device, we store that device's Android ID in a separate record, linked to your account, solely to detect and prevent multiple free-trial claims from the same device. This check does not apply on iOS.

3.2 Photos and Images You Upload

When you generate a remodel render, your uploaded photo is:

  • Held temporarily in your device RAM during your session
  • Transmitted securely to our server over HTTPS for AI processing
  • Processed in real time by Google Gemini AI
  • Immediately discarded after processing — not stored on our servers

3.3 Generated Renders and Design History

Your AI-generated renders are stored locally on your device:

  • Renders are private to your device
  • You can view and download renders offline
  • History keeps your 30 most recent renders
  • Renders older than 30 days are automatically removed from history
  • All local history is removed if you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in your device settings
  • Renders are NOT uploaded to our servers

3.4 Purchase Information

Purchases are processed natively by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS). We use RevenueCat to manage and validate purchases. We do not store your payment card details.

3.5 Device and Technical Information

Firebase and RevenueCat may automatically collect device identifiers, OS version, app version, and crash data.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Scape service
  • To process your uploaded photos and generate AI renders
  • To manage your credit balance and purchase history
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • To enable offline access to your design history
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API — Real-time AI processing. Photos processed and immediately discarded. Gemini API Terms
  • RevenueCat — Purchase management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Android payments. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — iOS payments. Apple Privacy Policy

6. Local Device Storage

  • Your 30 most recent AI-generated renders (renders older than 30 days are removed automatically), stored on your device for offline access
  • Active room photos in device RAM during your session
  • Anonymous session data stored locally until you sign in or clear app data

7. Data Storage and Security

Account metadata is stored securely using Google Firebase. Our server uses HTTPS encryption, Firebase App Check, server-side authentication, and Google Cloud Secret Manager.

8. Data Retention

  • Account metadata: Retained until you delete your account
  • Design renders: The 30 most recent are kept on your device; older entries and anything past 30 days are removed automatically, and all are removed if you uninstall the app
  • Uploaded photos: Not stored — processed and immediately discarded
  • Anonymous sessions: Stored locally until session ends or app is cleared
  • Purchase records: Retained 7 years for legal compliance

9. Your Rights

  • Access your data — email support.scapeapp@gmail.com
  • Delete your account — Settings then Delete Account in the app
  • Clear local history — uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage in your device settings, to remove all locally stored renders
  • Export your data — email support.scapeapp@gmail.com

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have additional rights under GDPR.

10. Children's Privacy

Scape is not directed at children under 13. Contact support.scapeapp@gmail.com if you believe a child has provided us information.

11. California Privacy Rights

We do not sell personal information. Contact support.scapeapp@gmail.com to exercise your CCPA rights.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Scape constitutes acceptance.
